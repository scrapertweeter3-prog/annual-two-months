The yearly rate hands back two months

A year of the Unlimited Software Development Subscription at Poket Dev runs $71,940 paid month to month at $5,995. On the yearly plan the same subscription prices at $4,995 a month, $59,940 over twelve. The gap is $12,000, which buys just over two months at the month-to-month rate, so a year's commitment leaves two of the twelve effectively unpaid.

The quarterly tier runs the same arithmetic smaller. Three months at $5,495 come to $16,485 against $17,985 at the month-to-month rate, a $1,500 refund per quarter. Four quarters return $6,000, a shade over one month of the $5,995 rate. Quarterly hands back about a month a year; yearly hands back two.

What the refunded months buy is the same workflow either way: unlimited request cards on a Trello board, one active at a time, finished code in a private repo you keep after canceling, revisions until you are satisfied, and no meetings. The commitment changes the bill, not the product. The full pricing table and the tier terms are at Poket Dev, same tiers at poketdev.com.
